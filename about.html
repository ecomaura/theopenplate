Our story

Open kitchen. Open plate.

The Open Plate is new this year, started by Rahul Jadhav and Hemant Gadam with 26 years of baking experience behind it. We run a cloud kitchen in Palava City making pastries and baked goods for homes, cafes and events, and we teach the craft in our Top Taste Kitchen class.

What we stand for

Fresh over fancy

Everything is baked to order. Nothing sits in a display for days.

Honest ingredients

Real butter and cream, proper chocolate, no artificial flavour shortcuts.

Share the craft

Our recipes are taught openly because every baker we train grows the community.
