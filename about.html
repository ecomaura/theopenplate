Our story

Open kitchen. Open plate.

We started in Dombivali with one oven and a belief that good pastry should not need a trip to South Mumbai. Today we run a full cloud kitchen supplying homes, cafes and event planners, and a classroom where anyone can learn the craft.

What we stand for

Fresh over fancy

Everything is baked to order. Nothing sits in a display for days.

Honest ingredients

Real butter and cream, proper chocolate, no artificial flavour shortcuts.

Share the craft
