Questions we hear most

How much notice do you need?

Three days for most cakes and pastries. Allow a week for large events and multi-tier cakes.

Do you make eggless or sugar-free bakes?

Yes, on request for many items. Tell us when you order and we will confirm what works.

Where do you deliver?

Dombivali, Kalyan, Thane and nearby suburbs. Pickup from our kitchen is by appointment.

Can I order for a cafe or office?

Yes. We supply daily and weekly standing orders with fixed pricing.

Ready to order or learn to bake?

Tell us the date, the guest count and the flavours you love. We reply with a clear quote.
