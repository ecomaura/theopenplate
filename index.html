Questions we hear most

How much notice do you need?

24 hours for cakes and pastries. Large events need more notice, so message us as early as you can.

Do you make eggless or sugar-free bakes?

Yes, we offer both. Tell us when you order and we will confirm the options for your bake.

Where do you deliver?

Mumbai and Pune. Pickup from our kitchen in Palava City is by appointment. Delivery charges follow our terms and conditions.

Can I order for a cafe or office?

Yes. We supply cafes and offices. Minimum order quantities follow our terms and conditions.

Ready to order or learn to bake?

Tell us the date, the guest count and the flavours you love. We reply with a clear quote.
